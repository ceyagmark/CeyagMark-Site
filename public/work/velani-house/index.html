Questions

Good to know.

How do I pay?

A 30% deposit by bank transfer holds your dates. The rest is paid on arrival, by card or cash.

Is the beach safe for swimming?

The cove below the house is calm from December to April. Outside those months we'll point you to the sheltered bay a short walk away.

Can we bring children?

Yes. The pool has no fence, so children need an adult with them near the water. Cots and high chairs are free.

How does the airport pickup work?

Send us your flight number. Our driver meets you at arrivals at Mattala or Colombo with a sign and cold king coconuts.
